To my Lawrenz, Happy 3rd monthsary, love. ♡ Three months with you, and you’re still someone I’m so happy to call mine. Thank you for being part of my days and for giving me little moments I can look back on and smile about. I hope you know that I’m always proud of you — of how hard you work, how you keep going, and of the person you are. I hope you give yourself time to rest, too, because you deserve softness and peace just as much as you deserve all the good things you’re working for. I miss you more than I can explain sometimes. If I could choose one little thing, it would be to spend more ordinary moments with you — talking, laughing, doing nothing, and just being together. Thank you for these three months, love. This is only a small page, but it carries a very big piece of my heart. I love you. Happy monthsary, Lawrenz. ♡ Always cheering for you, your girl, Francine
